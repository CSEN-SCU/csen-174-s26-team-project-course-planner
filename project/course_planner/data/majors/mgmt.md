**Prerequisites:** BUSN 70 or 170, and ECON 3. (4 units )

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** ACTG 11 and MKTG 181. (5 units)

### MGMT 170 — International Management

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** MGMT 80. MGMT 160 or 160S is recommended

Today’s business managers are challenged to set strategies, develop organizations, lead people, and control performance in a local context. They also need to understand and lead within a complex global context. This course is designed to help students: 1) understand multinational management; 2) recognize the challenges and opportunities presented by global markets; 3) implement a global strategy using organizational design, human resources management, and leadership development.
