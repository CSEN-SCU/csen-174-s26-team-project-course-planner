### MKTG 181 — Principles of Marketing

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** Must have completed 60 units or greater . (5 units)

Introduction to the fundamental principles of contemporary marketing. Covers the role of marketing in driving business and organizational success , marketing strategy and planning, segmentation , targeting, positioning, product policy, pricing decisions, promotion, and distribution. The course stresses topical examples. Emphasizes application of basic principles, information sourcing, analytical thinking, and communication skills.

### MKTG 182 — Analysis for Marketing Decisions

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** OMIS 41 or ECON 4 1 and MKTG 181 or 181S. (5 units)

An analytical approach toward understanding consumers and markets to support profitable marketing decisions in such areas as market segmentation, new product development, positioning, and promotions. The focus is on frameworks for structuring marketing problems, and techniques for using data to improve marketing decisions. Cases and projects are emphasized.

### MKTG 185 — Sales Management

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** MKTG 181 or 181S. (5 units)

This course puts the student in the role of being a prospective sales or marketing manager. The objective is to provide students with user-level knowledge of sales concepts and management methodologies necessary to effectively perform and manage the sales function. The format of the course enables the student to apply these concepts to selling both consumer high-tech and industrial products and services. Project required.

### MKTG 187 — Innovation and New Product Marketing

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** MKTG 181 or 181S. (5 units)

Focuses on both quantitative and qualitative techniques associated with identifying, researching, and analyzing new product opportunities. Exposes students to important tools for designing, testing, and introducing profitable new products and services.

### MKTG 189 — Sustainability Marketing

Units: 5

**Prerequisites:** MKTG 181 or 181S. (5 units)

The course is designed to explore the relationship between sustainability and marketing, especially for students interested in business and society and the environmental concerns that affect marketing managers. Key areas include understanding the economic foundation of sustainability marketing and its place in contemporary society, sustainability marketing standards and strategies, and global and ethical considerations.
